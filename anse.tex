		\textbf{Anse Poudrerie} (née Sarrah Rhausline) is a 28 year old female Rechia from Nova Casicha. She is an unregistered psion who utilized her powers for corporate espionage and has been living in exile since the Tachonyx affair.
		
		
		
		
		\section{Description}
	\end{strip}
	
	
	\subsection{Species}
	Rechia are a race of sapient warm-blooded humanoids who closely resemble Earth's arctic foxes (Vulpes lagopus). Though they evolved from herbivorous ancestors and still possess a proboscis-like tongue capable of harvesting plant sap and nectar from megaflora, their home planet Rechivar has been in an ice age for hundreds of thousands of years, and with the relative unavailability of flowering megaflora, they have evolved sharp front teeth and a wide and powerful jaw for scavenging and even hunting megafauna.
	In more seasonal climates, Rechia will grow a thick white coat in the winter and a lighter periwinkle coat in the summer, though since the onset of the ice age it has been rare to see Rechia on-world with blue fur. Rechia do not shed the hair that grows on the top and back of their head when seasonally molting, which results in striations in hair color between white and blue. Most off-world Rechia dye their hair as a result, though some take pride in maintaining their hair striations for as long as possible.
	
	\subsection{Speech}
	Rechian languages do not use velar consonants, as the thickness of Rechian proboscises makes the motion awkward. Both of Anse's parents spoke Coalition Standard English and modern Quebécois with a pronounced Rechian accent, and until around age 7 Anse did too. Anse learned Quebécois and English as a child, though because of the neighborhood she grew up in, she learned to speak English with a Quebécois accent, which still subtly persists in her speech, particularly in the substitution of post-alveolar consonants for alveolar and dental ones. She is soft-spoken, dry-witted, and shy, though under the effects of Bukorquitol she has been known to converse loudly and manically.

	\subsection{Early life}
	\subsubsection{Childhood}
	\indent Anse was born on 2375-05-28 to Rhaus Yvrehlen and Marne Wouthenline in the city of Nova Pliage on Nova Casicha. Her parents emigrated together in 2373 to Nova Casicha shortly before Rechivar's incorporation and the subsequent three year period of Rechian insurrection, arriving in the Quebec Nouveau district of Nova Pliage. Her parents quickly enmeshed themselves into the local population, adopting the leftist separatist politics and indifference to direct action along with a strong sense of Quebecer pride. Anse's birth was troubled, with Marne undergoing prolonged labor lasting three days with subsequent postpartum hemorrhage and infection that required a month-long hospitalization and treatment with narcotics, to which Marne would develop an addiction.\\
	\indent Anse was from a young age deeply immersed in neo-Quebecois culture and learned to speak Quebecois French before Coalition English. Her father, after only a few years in Nova Pliage, had become a pillar of the local community, founding and chairing the Quebec Nouveau Civic Association and leading the effort to have Nova Pliage formally recognize the Quebec Nouveau neighborhood on official maps. Anse was raised closely with other neighborhood children, and she and Rhaus would regularly socialize with other local families (particularly the family of Jean-Michel and Louise Gauthier, the former of which hired Rhaus as a Navigation Systems Developer at Tachonyx between 2377 and 2381 and supplied him with Bukorquitol) and participate in culturally significant events such as Révellion. Anse began learning the violin at age 4 for the purpose of playing rigaudon at festivities.\\
	\indent Marne was often absent, owing both to the odd work hours she endured as a nurse and her tendency to spend portions of her free time strung out on narcotics, particularly Parvocital. As such, Anse learned to do household work and care for herself at a young age, often being the only person present or sober in the house. Rhaus occasionally taught Anse how to cook recreationally and enjoyed cooking family meals, but was often busy himself with community organization and rare instances of  Bukorquitol indulgence and partying. However, on sober weekends, Marne was an attentive and caring mother. Anse often remembers childhood trips to the Harding Biodome in Crasita with both parents and time spent drawing or painting with her mother.\\
	\indent Between 2380 and 2382, Anse was molested five times by her mother. She has no memory of these events.\\
	\indent Anse was enrolled at PSNP24 beginning in 2378 and achieved consistently stellar grades, but struggled to make friends at school. She was well-liked but had trouble engaging socially and often spent time alone reading and tinkering with her father's leftover navigation hardware. When Anse expressed to Rhaus an interest in engineering, he gave her free reign of his workshop and taught her how to use his tools. Similarly, when (in her sober periods) Marne noticed Anse writing stories and poems of her own, she encouraged Anse by illustrating some of her works.\\
	\indent The two friendships Anse managed to foster as a young child were with other local Quebécois Nouveaux, namely Isabelle Gauthier (adopted niece of Louise Gauthier) and Quentin Zhyre. The three shared an interest in writing and would convene after school in the Old Quarter (a small cobblestone square lined with cafés and boutiques, constructed in 2370 ironically after most of Quebec Nouveau) and try to invent the most puzzling mystery setups they could devise.\\
	\subsubsection{Adolescence}
	\indent In 2386, minor redistricting meant Quentin was transferred out of PSNP24, although this did not hinder his friendship with Anse and Isabelle.\\
	\indent In 2387-10, Marne was diagnosed with a degenerative autoimmune disease unique to Rechia. She was given a grim prognosis with death estimated in the optimistic case within two years with treatment (and three months without). She was immediately put on paid leave and began a course of treatment to stall the disease's progression. That same day she went cold turkey on Parvocital (aided by her doctors with Methadone for withdrawal management) which made her demeanor aggressive and temperamental for a month, during which time she often verbally abused Anse and Rhaus. However, once the worst of withdrawal had passed, Marne returned to a period of lucid stability the likes of which Anse had never lived to see.\\
	\indent Although Marne collected leave pay for the duration of her sickness, the specificity of the disease-slowing treatment meant it was exorbitantly expensive, and to avoid falling too steeply into debt Rhaus picked up a second job bartending, working on average 70 hours a week. His presence in the house and in the local community diminished greatly, and as he coped with the stress his Bukorquitol usage became a dependency. For a few months Marne was able to manage her symptoms and take care of the house and of young Anse, during which time the pair kept each other in good spirits, but by 2388-03 her symptoms had progressed to where she was unable to stand, and it quickly became Anse's responsibility to take care of both herself and of Marne.\\
	\indent Having been a nurse for over a decade, Marne was uniquely capable of teaching Anse to manage her care, teaching her how to replace an IV, how to administer shots, how to clean and bandage lesions, and eventually how to change a bedpan, how to clean up diarrhea, and how to clean up vomited blood. By 2388-06 Marne could not be left in the house alone, and consequently Anse spent the summer solely as her mother's caretaker. The toll on Anse's grades had been severe, and when she was set to resume school in 2388-09, Rhaus made the decision to send Marne to a palliative treatment center.\\
	\indent Upon her return to school, Anse's grades slowly recovered, though her demeanor had become noticeably more cynical. Isabelle had acquired a shoplifting habit over the summer, which Anse encouraged her to apply to her father's liquor cabinet. The three began to drink recreationally and become known around Quebec Nouveau as troublemakers, and as both Anse and Isabelle reached puberty, they began to garner a reputation in PSNP24 as "bad girls". Both girls enjoyed the attention and began cultivating an edgier personal aesthetic, wearing dark makeup and revealing dark clothing. Quentin kept in step with the girls with a burgeoning interest in fashion that enabled him to dress the three of them in provocative style.\\
	\indent Marne's condition deteriorated significantly in the days leading up to Réveillon. On 2388-13-27, doctors reported that Marne was in critical condition, and Anse and Rhaus took short leave from school and work to be by Marne's bedside. The two remained by her bedside through Réveillon and the new year, eating from the waiting room [food maker thing] and not talking much, and by 2389-01-02 she seemed healthy enough for the two to return home. On the metro back home, the two got a call that Marne had suddenly passed away.\\
\indent Marne was cremated on 2389-01-06. Rhaus gave a speech that was half-inaudible through tears. Anse did not speak. She rarely spoke about her mother ever again.\\
\indent In 2389-05, Anse began to experience symptoms of psionic ability. She was plagued by migraines at school and in crowds, and began to occasionally read classmates' streams of consciousness, which she initially believed was schizophrenic audio hallucinations until an incident wherein she overheard another student's locker code and found that it worked. This would happen at random and often proved inconvenient, as Anse sometimes overheard classmates' and teachers' negative opinions of her. This led to a decrease in her mood and a tendency to isolate herself at school, becoming callous and withdrawn, and somewhat strained her relationship with Isabelle as Anse dropped out of popular favor and portions of the cool respect she'd garnered turned into fear. Over the next summer she remained largely alone except for occasional outings with Isabelle and Quentin, during the sober portions of which she trained herself to shut off what she heard from other people's minds, though by the next school year her cold attitude remained, garnering her (along with her pale blue fur) the cruel sobriquet Ice Queen.\\
\indent Although Rhaus gradually tapered his bartending as he whittled his dead wife's medical debt into a manageable debt repayment plan, his Bukorquitol usage did not slow down. He returned to community efforts and reimmersed himself in the community, learning indirectly of Anse's status as a local ruffian, though as a hands-off single parent he opted not to intercede (or even talk to her about it).\\
\indent In 2391-03, while looking for alcohol to pilfer from her father, Anse stumbled upon a baggie of his Bukorquitol. Recognizing it from contemporary media, she stole the baggie and brought it to Quentin and Isabelle. Quentin was as eager as Anse to get fucked up on it, while Isabelle thought it was a step too far, though ultimately she was convinced and the three began to regularly abuse Rhaus's Bukorquitol.\\

	\subsection{Young Adulthood}
	\indent Anse applied to college and successfully enrolled in Veduva Technical Institute in 2393 with a substantial merit scholarship. Though her grades were passable, her attendance was lacking, and early in her first semester her chronic Bukorquitol usage was reported to school officials by her roommate. She lost her merit scholarship immediately and was placed on a month's probation on the stipulation that a subsequent drug test at the end of the probationary period would return clean for Bukorquitol use or she would face expulsion. A month later, she failed her drug test and was expelled.\\
\indent Shortly after expulsion, Anse was approached by her godfather Jean-Michel Gauthier with an employment offer at Tachonyx in 2394-01. Anse was nominally hired as a lead communication technologies researcher on the Research and Development team, but within months of her hiring, Anse had been assigned to perform corporate espionage on Helios Astronautics' Research and Development team.

	\subsubsection{The Tachonyx affair}
\indent On 2397-04-14, within one week of the start of the federal investigation into suspected corporate espionage at Tachonyx, Anse disappeared from Nova Casicha with no word to family or colleagues. Authorities believe she was tipped off to the federal presence, but remain unclear how. Jean-Michel Gauthier suspects that Anse snitched on Tachonyx to the federal government and, after being sentenced to twelve years in a white-collar Nova Casichan correctional facility, instructed his men outside to find "Sarrah Rhausline", unaware of her present alias. Tachonyx was forced to liquidate.

	\subsection{Preferences}
	\subsubsection{Fashion}
	As a teenager, Anse opted for dark, revealing, and skintight outfits, like a black halter top and black miniskirt combo, or a black camisole paired with a red and black plaid miniskirt and a thick belt, or in cooler weather black leggings and a navy off-shoulder sweater. Once she reached adulthood her style matured, adding the occasional understated necklace or earring and swapping the plaid skirt and camisole for bodycon side-split dresses and black high-neck sweaters. Since exile, her style has changed substantially, wearing primarily understated earth tones, such as pale green cargo pants with a navy camisole and dark brown boots, and no jewelry or flowy clothing to remain as unnoticeable as possible.
	\subsubsection{Drugs}
\indent Since her early teens, Anse has enjoyed drinking alcohol to excess in social situations, but has never developed a dependency. In late teens Anse began abusing her father's Bukorquitol and after a year she had become a high-functioning addict. She has tried other drugs and enjoyed other uppers but does not like downers apart from alcohol.
\subsubsection{Cuisine}
\indent Anse has never had much of a sweet tooth and prefers to eat spicy or heavily seasoned savory foods. She enjoys authentic meat and fish but only rarely eats it.\\
\indent 	As for alcohol, she prefers dry white wines and simple gin cocktails.
\subsection{Hobbies}
\indent Anse enjoys recreational engineering, puzzles, reading fiction and poetry, and writing poetry. She also enjoys playing fiddle when one is available.

\subsection{Fears}
Anse has a mild phobia of deep oceans and lives in fear of assassination attempts on behalf of Gauthier.
