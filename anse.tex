         \textbf{Anse Poudrerie} (née Sarrah Rhausline) is a 28 year old female Rechia from Nova Casicha. She is an unregistered psion who utilized her powers for corporate espionage and has been living in exile since the Tachonyx affair.




         \section{Description}
         \end{strip}


         \subsection{Species}
         Rechia are a race of sapient warm-blooded humanoids who closely resemble Earth's arctic foxes (Vulpes lagopus). Though they evolved from herbivorous ancestors and still possess a proboscis-like tongue capable of harvesting plant sap and nectar from megaflora, their home planet Rechivar has been in an ice age for hundreds of thousands of years, and with the relative unavailability of flowering megaflora, they have evolved sharp front teeth and a wide and powerful jaw for scavenging and even hunting megafauna.
         In more seasonal climates, Rechia will grow a thick white coat in the winter and a lighter periwinkle coat in the summer, though since the onset of the ice age it has been rare to see Rechia on-world with blue fur. Rechia do not shed the hair that grows on the top and back of their head when seasonally molting, which results in striations in hair color between white and blue. Most off-world Rechia dye their hair as a result, though some take pride in maintaining their hair striations for as long as possible.


         \subsection{Speech}
         Rechian languages do not use velar consonants, as the thickness of Rechian proboscises makes the motion awkward. Both of Anse's parents spoke Coalition Standard English and modern Quebécois with a pronounced Rechian accent, and until around age 7 Anse did too. Anse learned Quebécois and English as a child, though because of the neighborhood she grew up in, she learned to speak English with a Quebécois accent, which still subtly persists in her speech. She is soft-spoken, dry-witted, and shy, though under the effects of Bukorquitol she has been known to converse loudly and manically.

         \subsection{Early life}
         Anse was born in 2375 to Rhaus Yvrehlen and Marne Wouthenline in the city of Nova Pliage on Nova Casicha. Her parents emigrated together in 2373 to Nova Casicha shortly before Rechivar's incorporation and the subsequent three year period of Rechian insurrection, arriving in the Quebec Nouveau district of Nova Pliage. Her parents quickly enmeshed themselves into the local population, adopting the leftist separatist politics and indifference to direct action along with a strong sense of Quebecer pride. Anse's birth was troubled, with Marne undergoing prolonged labor lasting three days with subsequent postpartum hemorrhage and infection that required a month-long hospitalization and treatment with narcotics, to which Marne would develop an addiction.\\

         Anse applied to college and successfully enrolled in Veduva Technical Institute in 2393 with a substantial merit scholarship. Though her grades were passable, early in her first semester her Bukorquitol usage was reported to school officials by her roommate. She lost her merit scholarship immediately and was placed on a month's probation on the stipulation that a subsequent drug test at the end of the probationary period would return clean for Bukorquitol use or she would face expulsion. A month later, she failed her drug test and was expelled.


         \subsection{Corporate espionage}
         Shortly after expulsion, Anse was approached by her godfather Jean-Michel Gauthier with an employment offer at Tachonyx in winter of 2394. Anse was nominally hired as a lead researcher on the Research and Development, but within months of her hiring, Anse had been assigned to perform corporate espionage on Helios Astronautics' Research and Development team. Evidence presented during the Tachonyx affair indicates that Anse infiltrated the Helios Brighton research labs on multiple occasions under different aliases between autumn of 2394 and summer of 2397.
         Within one week of the start of the federal investigation into reports of espionage, Anse disappeared from Nova Casicha with no word to family or colleagues. Authorities suspect she was tipped off to the federal presence, but remain unclear how. At no point in the investigation did Jean-Michel Gauthier disclose any knowledge of Anse's psionic abilities.
